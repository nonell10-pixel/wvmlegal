WORKFLOW VIDEO MASTER

A private desktop tool for creators to produce, review and publish short-form video.

What it is

WORKFLOW VIDEO MASTER is a private, locally-run macOS application that helps an individual creator turn their own long-form footage into short-form videos, review them, and prepare or upload them to the creator's own social channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok). It runs on the creator's own computer; it is not a public web service.

How it uses platform APIs

The application connects to the creator's own accounts through the official YouTube, Instagram (Meta) and TikTok APIs, with the creator's explicit authorization, only to:

  • upload the creator's own videos to the creator's own channels, and
  • read the creator's own analytics (views and related metrics) for those videos.

The application does not access other users' data, does not post on behalf of anyone other than the account owner, and never publishes without the owner's manual approval.
