Privacy Policy

Application: WORKFLOW VIDEO MASTER · Last updated: 19 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WORKFLOW VIDEO MASTER ("the App", "we") handles information. The App is operated by Christian Nonell Davila. For any question, contact neloracorp@gmail.com.

1. What the App is

The App is a private application that runs locally on the user's own computer. It helps the user create short-form videos from their own footage and publish them to the user's own social media channels. The App is used by the account owner for their own accounts; it is not offered as a public, multi-user online service.

2. Information we access

With the user's explicit authorization through official platform sign-in (OAuth), the App accesses:

  • Account authorization tokens for the user's own YouTube, Instagram and/or TikTok accounts, used to upload the user's content and read the user's own metrics.
  • The user's own content and analytics (videos the user uploads through the App, and the views and performance metrics of those videos).

The App does not collect data about other people, does not access contacts, and does not track users across other websites or apps.

3. How information is stored

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on the user's own device (encrypted at rest; the encryption key is held in the operating system keychain). Tokens are never written to logs, never displayed back after being saved, and never shared in source control. The user can remove stored credentials at any time from within the App.

4. Third-party services

The App communicates only with services needed to perform the user's requested actions:

  • YouTube / Google, Meta (Instagram) and TikTok APIs — to upload the user's videos and read the user's analytics.
  • AI providers (for example, OpenAI) — to help generate titles, thumbnails, scripts or voice for the user's own content, when the user enables those features.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

5. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using these features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed via YouTube API Services is handled in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via Google account permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored locally, the user controls retention. Removing a connection in the App, or deleting the App and its local data, removes the stored tokens from the device. To revoke platform access, the user can also remove the App from their YouTube/Google, Meta or TikTok account settings.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is intended for the account owner's professional or personal creator use.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will reflect any change.
